# Scrap Mechanic Field Guide

[Scrap Mechanic Field Guide](https://scrapmechanic.org) is an independent player resource for builders, explorers, farmers, and returning mechanics. It brings practical guides, a connected wiki, tested build plans, update coverage, mod advice, and calculation tools into one place.

The site is written for players who want to solve a problem in their current world: finding the right material, understanding a crafting chain, preparing for a raid, planning a vehicle, checking a patch change, or deciding whether a mod is safe to add.

## About Scrap Mechanic

Scrap Mechanic is a mechanical sandbox game developed by Axolot Games. Players combine structural blocks with bearings, engines, controllers, sensors, logic parts, pistons, thrusters, and other interactive components to create working machines.

Survival adds a broader progression loop around that building system. Mechanics gather and refine resources, operate crafting stations, grow crops, defend farms, trade packed produce, complete quests, explore generated worlds, and fight hostile bots. Creative mode removes much of the progression pressure and provides room for rapid prototyping, logic experiments, vehicles, automated systems, and large collaborative creations.

## Core Game Features

- **Mechanical building:** Create cars, mining vehicles, farm machines, defenses, aircraft, factories, and experimental contraptions from connected parts.
- **Logic and automation:** Use sensors, controllers, switches, logic gates, timers, pistons, bearings, and vacuum systems to automate repeated jobs.
- **Survival progression:** Collect materials, unlock recipes, upgrade crafting stations, grow crops, trade produce, complete quests, and prepare for increasingly dangerous encounters.
- **Farming and raids:** Balance crop value, field size, defenses, ammunition, and repair capacity before a raid begins.
- **Exploration:** Follow roads and landmarks through generated Survival worlds to locate stations, ruins, traders, warehouses, resources, and quest objectives.
- **Combat and bots:** Learn enemy behavior, drops, attack patterns, and safer ways to handle raids or dangerous exploration routes.
- **Creative experimentation:** Prototype mechanisms without the resource constraints of Survival and refine them before rebuilding in a progression world.
- **Community content:** Extend the game through Steam Workshop creations and mods while paying attention to compatibility and save risk.

## Recommended Starting Points

New players can begin with the [first-hours Survival guide](https://scrapmechanic.org/guides/beginner-first-hours), which focuses on early priorities instead of asking players to learn every system at once. Returning players should use the [returning-to-1.0 guide](https://scrapmechanic.org/guides/returning-to-1-0) before making irreversible changes to an older world.

For progression planning, these pages connect the most common decisions:

- [Farming basics](https://scrapmechanic.org/guides/farming-basics) explains crop planning, field preparation, and the relationship between farming and raids.
- [Scrap Mechanic recipes](https://scrapmechanic.org/wiki/recipes) lists crafting results, required inputs, quantities, and stations.
- [Trader prices](https://scrapmechanic.org/wiki/trades) helps compare current offers and distinguish finished-item trades from recipe unlocks.
- [Quest list](https://scrapmechanic.org/wiki/quests) keeps objectives, requirements, rewards, and recovery advice together.
- [Raid Calculator](https://scrapmechanic.org/tools/raid-calculator) turns crop value and field choices into a clearer defense estimate.
- [Crafting Planner](https://scrapmechanic.org/tools/crafting-planner) totals direct materials and recipe requirements before a large crafting run.

## How to Use the Wiki

Start with the object or system you already know. An item page can lead to its recipe, related resources, trades, quests, and practical uses. Category directories make it easier to compare similar objects instead of opening unrelated pages one at a time.

The wiki is designed around player decisions rather than isolated definitions. A useful entry should help answer questions such as:

- What does this item or part do?
- How is it obtained, crafted, unlocked, or traded?
- Which station, resource, quest, or schematic is connected to it?
- Where does it fit into a vehicle, farm, defense, or automation system?
- Has the relevant information been separated from older or incompatible game behavior?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Scrap Mechanic Field Guide an official website?

No. [scrapmechanic.org](https://scrapmechanic.org) is an independent fan-made player resource. Scrap Mechanic and its related names, artwork, and game assets belong to their respective rights holders.

### Does the site cover Survival or Creative mode?

It covers both, but many wiki entries, guides, calculators, quests, recipes, trades, and progression notes are focused on Survival. Creative-specific advice is identified where the game rules or available tools differ.

### Where should a new player begin?

Open the [Guides](https://scrapmechanic.org/guides) section and start with the first-hours route. It prioritizes shelter, basic tools, resource flow, crafting access, mobility, food, and early farm decisions.

### Can I use the map to find an exact location?

The [Map](https://scrapmechanic.org/map) page includes an embedded interactive world viewer. Choose among 18 surface, story, Grow Lab, underground, and boss-region views, then use location search, terrain and coordinate layers, and player markers to plan a route. Generated roads, ruins, resources, and other locations can differ between Survival worlds, so confirm the destination in your own save instead of relying only on coordinates copied from another player.

### How do I calculate the raid created by my crops?

Use the [Raid Calculator](https://scrapmechanic.org/tools/raid-calculator). Enter the relevant crop and field information, then review the estimated raid level and bot pressure before planting or expanding a farm.

### How can I total materials for a large crafting job?

Use the [Crafting Planner](https://scrapmechanic.org/tools/crafting-planner). It is intended for checking recipe quantities and material requirements before loading a Craftbot or committing stored resources.

### Are mods safe to add to an existing save?

Compatibility varies by mod and game version. Read the [Mods](https://scrapmechanic.org/mods) guidance, check the Workshop page and recent player reports, and back up an important save before changing its mod list.

### What should I do before opening an old save after an update?

Read the relevant [Updates](https://scrapmechanic.org/updates) page first and make a backup. Update notes can identify save boundaries, changed systems, known fixes, and areas that deserve extra caution.

### How can I report incorrect information or a broken link?

Visit the [Contact Us](https://scrapmechanic.org/contact) page or email [wyong@scrapmechanic.org](mailto:wyong@scrapmechanic.org) with the affected URL and a clear description of the issue.
